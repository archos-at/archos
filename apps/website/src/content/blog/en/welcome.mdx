Architects shape our future – yet the knowledge it takes is scattered across guidelines,
state building codes, project files and the experience of your own office.

ARCHOS connects project data, building law, budget and office knowledge in one place. In
this blog we share what we learn along the way.

## What to expect

The blog has three sections:

- **[Product features](/en/blog/product-features/)**: what ARCHOS does, how an office puts
  it to work, and what is new.
- **[From practice](/en/blog/from-practice/)**: how planning firms put AI to good use today
  without giving up control, and what holds up day to day.
- **[Technology](/en/blog/technology/)**: how ARCHOS is built and why, how an answer
  becomes traceable to its source, and what we learn from mistakes.
