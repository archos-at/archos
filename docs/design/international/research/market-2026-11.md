# Market research: AI for planning offices and building law

Prepared 2026-11-10 for the international base-knowledge rollout (Austria first, then Germany and the EU).

**Evidence window:** every source cited below was published or updated on or after 2026-06-10, except the UpCodes press release of 2026-06-03, which the PR review found outside it; it is marked where cited and kept as context only. Each claim carries its URL and date. Vendor pages and vendor-run benchmarks are marked as such. A section at the end lists the sources I dropped and why.

**What {productName} is:** the workspace for architects, as [`VISION.md`](../../../../VISION.md) defines it. Building law is one of its capabilities. The market below is mostly made of single-purpose tools (code research, drawing QA/QC, legal research). The comparison judges {productName} on the capability these tools share with it. It does not judge {productName} as a compliance assistant.

---

## Summary

1. **The market splits into four groups.**
   - **US drawing and model checkers:** UpCodes Plan Review, Kestrel, Archiboost, Nomic, UptoCode, InspectMind, Blueprints AI.
   - **German publisher chatbots for Baurecht:** WEKA Bau AI, BaurechtGPT, K24AI.
   - **Legal-AI workspaces built on publisher content:** Beck-Noxtua, MANZ-Noxtua, Lexis+ mit Protégé.
   - **CAD and office suites adding agents and MCP:** Archicad Max, Allplan Max, Autodesk Assistant, Phase0, PlanRadar.

   No group combines the office's own files, the project, the model and the law in one workspace.
2. **Austria is thin on the ground.** Only K24AI says it covers OIB, ÖNORM and DACH law, and that claim is undated and unverified. Baubescheid+ covers NÖ only and sells to municipalities. MANZ-Noxtua and Lexis Protégé cover Austrian law for lawyers, with no Baurecht focus documented.
3. **Which edition is binding is the hard problem, and it is Austria's defining trait.** The OIB's own table (Stand Oktober 2026) shows the nine Länder on three editions. Steiermark, Burgenland and Vorarlberg are still on 2019. Salzburg moved RL 1–5 to 2023 on 06.08.2026. Wien and Tirol moved RL 6 to 2025 in July 2026. No competitor documents how it decides which edition is in force on a given day.
4. **What practitioners ask for:** answers traceable to the passage, the edition stated, an audit trail of who decided what, honest abstention, and integration with their tools. They distrust confident wrong citations, missing state variants, and tools that "carry no professional liability".
5. **Price anchors in DACH run from €33 to €49 per user per month** for building-law chat. At the published caps, that works out to **€0.05–0.43 per prompt**, which bounds our per-answer cost.

## (b) Five things the best competitors do that we should match or beat

**1. Fix the jurisdiction and edition once per project, and scope every answer to it.**
UpCodes Plan Review: "users set the jurisdiction, code year, and building type once per project", and every analysis runs against that adoption ([2026-06-03, **outside the window**, kept as context only](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/upcodes-adds-ai-native-plan-review-to-its-aec-qaqc-platform-302789640.html)). Its runs already span "14 code editions" ([2026-10-13](https://www.aol.com/articles/upcodes-ai-reviews-1-300-100000000.html)). The AEC Magazine buyer's guide tells firms to ask "which regions, jurisdictions and editions are covered" and how fast coverage follows an amendment ([AEC Magazine, 2026-10-22/27](https://aecmag.com/cad/compliance-software-in-aec)).
*Match or beat:* the Einrichtung already fixes Bundesland and GK. Add the **reference day**: the Einreichdatum, or the planned one. Every legal answer resolves the edition on that day. Bauguide's advice on OIB-RL 6 is "Projektstand entscheidend: Einreichdatum, Verfahrensart … bestimmen, welche Fassung gilt" ([bauguide.at, 2026, cites the OIB table Stand Juli 2026](https://www.bauguide.at/oib-richtlinie-6-wien-tirol-2026/)).

**2. Tie every finding to an element or page and to a clause, and make it a tracked issue that survives revisions.**
UpCodes issues can be filtered, commented, assigned and tracked, re-run after revision and exported as a report ([2026-06-03, **outside the window**, kept as context only](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/upcodes-adds-ai-native-plan-review-to-its-aec-qaqc-platform-302789640.html)). Kestrel ties each result to the model element and the section ([2026-06-11](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kestrel-labs-unveils-the-first-ai-powered-compliance-platform-built-natively-inside-bim-302798153.html)). UptoCode does the same for IFC ([2026-07-09](https://uptocode.ai/blog/best-ai-building-code-compliance-software-2026)). AEC Magazine argues that without a record of who raised an issue, what was decided and why, "a compliance report … is a document that has just been received, rather than a check that has been carried out" ([2026-10-22](https://aecmag.com/cad/compliance-software-in-aec)).
*Match or beat:* the Prüfbuch and BCF already cover the model. Extend them to drawings and documents, and add a diff across revisions: new, resolved and still-open findings.

**3. Show the passage under the claim, with publisher-grade depth behind it.**
Lexis Protégé AT lets the user expand "die maßgeblichen Textpassagen … unter der jeweiligen Aussage" ([2026-09-17](https://www.ots.at/presseaussendung/OTS_20260917_OTS0139/lexisnexis-setzt-auf-neue-agentic-ai-technologie-fuer-recht-steuer)). Beck-Noxtua added IBR's Baurecht content ([2026-07-06](https://www.juve.de/markt-und-management/beck-noxtua-integriert-inhalte-von-acht-weiteren-verlagen/)). WEKA answers from about 20,000 edited articles and says when it cannot answer ([Oct 2026](https://flinki.ai/tools/weka-bau-ai)).
*Match or beat:* citations at the passage already exist. We lack commentary and interpretive content (Kommentar, Erläuterungen, Auslegungen of the authorities, Erläuternde Bemerkungen to the OIB-RL). Austrian Standards "Norman" ([2026-09-15](https://www.ots.at/presseaussendung/OTS_20260915_OTS0090/normenschaffung-fit-fuer-die-zukunft-machen)) shows that the ÖNORM owner will sell AI over standards. Decide on licensing or partnering early.

**4. Work where the architect already works, through open agent connectivity.**
Kestrel runs inside Revit. UpCodes shipped code research in Revit ([AEC Hub, July 2026](https://www.aechub.org/insights/latest-aec-ai-tech)). Archicad Max ships a public MCP server (beta), and Bluebeam Max supports MCP for Claude ([2026-10-29](https://www.nemetschek.com/en/news-media/ngroup-brings-ai-to-aec-with-max-releases)). Autodesk's Assistant Builder connects customers' own agents ([2026-09-15](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/autodesk-advances-agentic-ai-in-its-three-industry-clouds-302877889.html)). One in three Chaos survey respondents complain about poor integration. That survey is dated 2026-05-20, so it is outside the window and is cited here as background only, not as evidence.
*Match or beat:* the our MCP server and the BCF round-trip exist today. The beat is to *consume* the Archicad MCP server: read the live model, not only IFC exports.

**5. Simple, predictable pricing and EU-sovereign operation.**
Kestrel sells an annual firm-wide licence with no per-seat fee and unlimited checks ([2026-06-11](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kestrel-labs-unveils-the-first-ai-powered-compliance-platform-built-natively-inside-bim-302798153.html)). WEKA charges a flat €499/yr ([Oct 2026](https://flinki.ai/tools/weka-bau-ai)). MANZ-Noxtua runs in European data centres with ISO 27001, ISO 42001 and BSI C5 ([2026-06-24](https://www.manz.at/unternehmen/presse/newsroom/2026/souveraene-europaeische-rechts-ki-fuer-oesterreich-manz-startet-legal-ai-workspace-manz-noxtua)). Lexis Protégé is EU-hosted and does not train on customer data ([2026-09-17](https://www.ots.at/presseaussendung/OTS_20260917_OTS0139/lexisnexis-setzt-auf-neue-agentic-ai-technologie-fuer-recht-steuer)). Reviewers mark down US sub-processors (Phase0) and observability data that may leave the EU (WEKA) ([ki-syndikat, Nov 2026](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/ki-tools/bauwesen/); [flinki, Oct 2026](https://flinki.ai/tools/weka-bau-ai)).
*Match or beat:* publish the hosting region, the sub-processors and certification status on the pricing page. Price without per-question anxiety. Avoid prompt caps like beck-chat's 50 per day.

## (c) Where we are already ahead

The {productName} claims below come from [`VISION.md`](../../../../VISION.md) and the code I checked.

1. **Edition in force per Land, failing closed.** `apps/workspace/bff/base-knowledge/time/editions.ts` resolves "which edition of a work was in force on a day" (ADR-0171). It returns `history_not_held`, `edition_dates_unknown` or `ambiguous_editions` instead of guessing. `configs/knowledge-packs/at.json` holds 135 `makes_binding` relations per Land, with LGBl provision and validity dates. I spot-checked them against the OIB table (Stand Okt 2026) ([oib.or.at](https://www.oib.or.at/kernaufgaben/oib-richtlinien/)). They agree with it except where the authentic gazette on RIS differs, which governs (Salzburg, Niederösterreich, Burgenland):
   - Salzburg RL 1–5 2023 from 2026-09-01 (LGBl. 83/2026 § 9 Abs 1; the OIB table's 2026-08-06 is the publication day, and the pack follows RIS since 2026-11-10);
   - Tirol RL 6 2025 from 2026-07-16;
   - Wien RL 6 2025 from 2026-07-15;
   - Steiermark still on 2019.

   **No in-window competitor source documents edition-on-a-day handling.** UpCodes has a per-project code year. WEKA and K24AI say nothing.
2. **One workspace: office knowledge, project files, model and law together.** The competitors are research tools (WEKA, K24AI, UpCodes Copilot), drawing checkers (UpCodes Plan Review, Archiboost, UptoCode) or legal workspaces for lawyers (Noxtua, Protégé). Phase0 (AVA and HOAI) and PlanRadar (site work) are the nearest office platforms, and neither carries building law. Nemetschek's "Assistant Spaces" grounded in firm standards ([2026-10-29](https://www.nemetschek.com/en/news-media/ngroup-brings-ai-to-aec-with-max-releases)) show the direction, but they ship no code knowledge.
3. **IFC check that says what is missing to decide.** Per VISION, each rule passes, fails, or names the property the model would have to publish, with threshold and clause. Results go to the Prüfbuch and back to ArchiCAD or Revit as BCF. Kestrel works only in Revit and UptoCode only on IFC. Neither documents an "undecidable, here's why" state or a confirmation ledger.
4. **Suggest-then-confirm with a recorded decision.** Freigabe and the Prüfbuch already provide the audit trail that AEC Magazine ([2026-10-22](https://aecmag.com/cad/compliance-software-in-aec)) and PlanOps ("the liability didn't move just because the checking did", [2026-09-11](https://planops.ai/insights/weekly-roundup/2026-09-11-weekly-roundup)) say buyers need.
5. **An MCP door for the office's own agents, live today.** Archicad's MCP server is beta ([2026-10-29](https://www.nemetschek.com/en/news-media/ngroup-brings-ai-to-aec-with-max-releases)). Autodesk's Builder arrives in 2027 ([2026-09-15](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/autodesk-advances-agentic-ai-in-its-three-industry-clouds-302877889.html)).

**Gaps that undercut the lead:**
- **Past-day Land law from RIS.** `packages/ris-adapter/src/lookup/as_of.py` notes: "For Land law the record names no date parameter [verify the OGD API], so a Land search may return only today's versions." It fails closed, but that means a miss for a project submitted before an amendment.
- **RIS reliability.** RIS was slowed in August 2026 by "automated requests from AI-driven bots" ([ORF, 2026-08-20](https://orf.at/stories/3439709/)). Ingestion must be cached and polite, never live per question.
- **A discrepancy to settle on RIS.** Bauguide cites "§ 33 der Technischen Bauvorschrift" for Tirol's RL 6 adoption ([bauguide.at](https://www.bauguide.at/oib-richtlinie-6-wien-tirol-2026/)). ## 2. What practitioners say they need, and what makes them distrust a tool

These are in-window sources only.

- **Verification remains on the professional, so tools must make checking cheap.**
  - "None of these products carry professional liability … Professional responsibility still sits with you" ([AEC Magazine, 2026-10-22](https://aecmag.com/cad/compliance-software-in-aec)).
  - "The liability didn't move just because the checking did." Ask "which standards an AI reviewer checked against and who signed the output" ([PlanOps, 2026-09-11](https://planops.ai/insights/weekly-roundup/2026-09-11-weekly-roundup)).
  - Nemetschek: "AI proposes … professionals review, decide and remain accountable" ([2026-10-29](https://www.nemetschek.com/en/news-media/ngroup-brings-ai-to-aec-with-max-releases)).
- **Prescriptive checks are trusted. Performance requirements are not.** Dimensions and U-values can be checked. Means of escape needs "modelling, judgement and a competent person" ([AEC Magazine, 2026-10-22](https://aecmag.com/cad/compliance-software-in-aec); [ENGtechnica, 2026-10-27](https://engtechnica.com/ai-compliance-tools-are-changing-aec-design-reviews/)).
- **Test a tool on a known project and count what it misses.** ENGtechnica recommends testing against a completed project with known problems ([2026-10-27](https://engtechnica.com/ai-compliance-tools-are-changing-aec-design-reviews/)). PlanOps recommends trialling on a hand-reviewed set ([2026-09-11](https://planops.ai/insights/weekly-roundup/2026-09-11-weekly-roundup)).
- **The German profession wants rules on traceability.** The BAK KI-Kodex topics are responsibility and a duty to check, data provenance, and results that can be reconstructed in disputes; a workshop was held in June 2026 ([Phase0, updated 2026-11-05](https://www.phase0.com/blog/ki-kodex-architektur)). The AI Act's transparency duties apply from 2026-08-02 ([legal-tech.de, 2026-09-23](https://legal-tech.de/zwoelf-ki-tools-fuer-kanzleien/)).
- **Offices are under economic pressure.**
  - 60% of German offices report longer permit planning, and 32% make no surplus ([architekturblatt, 2026-10-29](https://www.architekturblatt.de/strukturbefragung-architektenkammern-2026/)).
  - US firms: 48% engage with AI, but only 22% of users report a substantial productivity gain, and 15% have a dedicated AI budget ([AIA ABI, 2026-09-23](https://www.aia.org/resource-center/abi-august-2026-architecture-firm-billings-continue-decline)).

  Value has to be visible in hours saved, and cost has to be predictable.
- **Data sovereignty is a buying criterion in DACH.** Reviewers flag US sub-processors and observability that may leave the EU ([ki-syndikat, Nov 2026](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/ki-tools/bauwesen/); [flinki, Oct 2026](https://flinki.ai/tools/weka-bau-ai)).
- **Gap in the evidence.** I found no in-window first-hand DACH user complaints (Reddit, LinkedIn, Bauwelt, Architektur & Bau Forum) about hallucinated paragraphs or wrong editions. The strongest DACH statements on that are from before the window and were dropped. Practitioner interviews would close this gap better than more desk research.

**What this implies for our per-answer cost:**
- **The DACH anchor for a building-law assistant is about €33–49 per user per month.** Building law is one capability inside {productName}, not the whole product, so its cost has to fit well inside that.
- **At K24AI's cap, a prompt is worth about €0.05.** At BaurechtGPT's, about €0.43.
- **A heavy user** asking about 15 legal questions per working day (about 300 a month) at €0.05 costs €15 a month in model spend. That is affordable inside a €40–80 seat. At €0.36 per answer (a frontier-model figure from a vendor benchmark), the same user costs €108, which is not affordable without caps.
- **Hence R14:** median ≤ €0.05 for a routine legal answer, with deep research (Tiefenrecherche) metered or bundled separately.
- **Avoid caps users feel.** Competitors show that prompt caps (75/mo, 50/day) are the visible cost lever. Kestrel and WEKA advertise *no* cap as the feature.
- **Measure, don't assume.** `configs/config_tokenomics_pricing.yml` still prices `gpt-5.2` while the configs reference `gpt-6-luna`. Take our real figure from the answer suite's cost-per-answer report, not from this table.
